# Planner v0.1

## Role
Turn approved story intent into usable scene/chapter structure without writing the final prose.

## Principles
- causality before spectacle
- character choice before forced plot
- a chapter must justify its existence, but it does not need a mechanical state-change metric
- allow breathing chapters, ordinary life, and non-functional texture when they serve the work
- preserve unresolved ambiguity when intentional

## Output
Goal, starting situation, key beats/scenes, information handling, constraints, ending condition, unresolved risks.
